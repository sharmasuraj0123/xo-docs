**XO Research** · July 2026 (v3)

**TL;DR:** quirqs are only as real as the machinery beneath them. The
environment is that machinery: it captures the before- and after-state, meters
every action, and writes the tamper-evident record, which is why verification
can never be gamed by the worker it verifies. The full canonical treatment
(calculus, dashboard, gaming, validation, playbook) lives in the
[quirq section](/quirq); this note carries the environment argument and hands
the chain onward.
