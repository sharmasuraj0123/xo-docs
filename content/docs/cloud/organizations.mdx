<Lead>
  An organization is a team's workspace on XO Cloud. Switch to **Org Spaces** in
  the sidebar's scope switcher to work in it: chat with its agent and, as an
  owner, decide who joins.
</Lead>

## What an organization is

On **New Space**, the **Orgs** tab, **Launch Your AI Operations**, explains how it
will work, marked **Coming Soon**: create your org, add your team, bring in agents,
design the structure, open your offices and set the rules.

![Launch Your AI Operations: How it works, and What you get](/images/cloud/create-space-orgs.png)

## Chat with the organization's agent

1. In the scope switcher at the top of the sidebar, choose **Org Spaces**.
2. The sidebar lists the organization's chats, grouped by **Today** and **Previous
   7 days**. **New chat** starts one; **Refresh history** reloads the list.
3. Type in **Ask the agent something…** and send. The picker beside the input
   chooses which project the message is about.

![The organization Argus with no chat open: its chats in the sidebar, Create and Manage Issues, and the message box highlighted](/images/cloud/org.png)

In a chat, the agent's answer shows the steps it took, such as reading a file.

![An organization chat: a question, the agent reading PLAN.md, and its answer](/images/cloud/org-chat.png)

The bar along the top shows the organization (for example **Org › Argus**), whether
it is **Ready** and its **API** is **Up**, **Stop**, **Space apps** and
**Connections** menus, and **Settings**.

## Manage members

Choose **Settings** in the top bar. Under **Members**:

- **Join requests**: approve people who asked to join. Approving one sets up that
  person's workspace, which takes a moment. "No pending requests" when there are none.
- **Members**: everyone in the organization, with their email, role (for example
  **Owner**) and agent.

**Danger zone** is the other section of Settings.

![Argus Settings, highlighted: Members, with no pending join requests and Alex Rivera as Owner](/images/cloud/org-settings.png)
