<Lead>
  XO Cloud shows what your agents use: cost, tokens, messages and latency. See it
  for one space on that space's **Usage** page, or for all spaces on **Usage** in the
  sidebar.
</Lead>

## Across all spaces

Choose **Usage** in the sidebar. **Spaces Usage** covers the period you choose
(**Last 7 days** by default):

- Totals: **TOTAL COST**, **TOTAL TOKENS**, **TOTAL MESSAGES** and **AVG LATENCY**.
- **Tokens over time**: daily token volume across all spaces.
- **Daily Cost**: actual spend per day across all spaces.
- **Message Breakdown**: messages by role, **User**, **Asst** (assistant) and **Tools**.
- **Performance**: latency over time, minimum, average, p95 and maximum.

![Usage across all spaces: the four totals, Tokens over time, Daily Cost, Message Breakdown and Performance](/images/cloud/usage.png)

The dashboard's **Usage Summary** shows the same cost, messages and tokens in short.

## For one space

Open the space's **Usage** page. It has the same totals for this space only, and
**Tokens over time**.

![A space's Usage page with its totals and Tokens over time](/images/cloud/space-usage.png)
