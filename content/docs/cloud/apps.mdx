<Lead>
  A space's apps open in the panel on the right of its page. **Space** and
  **Cowork** are the full XO interfaces for this space; **Claude** and **Codex**
  manage the account the agent runs on.
</Lead>

## Space and Cowork

Choose **Space** or **Cowork** in the app grid. The app opens in the panel with its
own address bar, **Reload**, **Open … in a new tab** and a home button. Everything
in the [XO Space guide](/docs/space/space-walk) and the [Cowork guide](/docs/cowork)
applies to it.

![The Space app open in a space's panel, showing XO Space's Projects overview](/images/cloud/app-space.png)

![The Cowork app open in a space's panel, showing Cowork's Home](/images/cloud/app-cowork.png)

To work with more room, choose **Open in a new tab**.

## Claude

In a Claude Code space, **Claude** shows:

- **AUTHENTICATION**: your **Claude Account** and whether it is **Connected**. When
  it isn't, **Connect your Claude subscription** and **Connect** sign it in.
- **REMOTE SESSION**: whether the agent's session is **Running**, with **Open
  Session** and **Stop**.

![The Claude app in a space's panel, highlighted: the Claude Account connected, and a running remote session with Open Session and Stop](/images/cloud/app-claude-pane.png)

See [Claude OAuth](/docs/cloud/setup/models/oauth/claude) for connecting a Claude
subscription.

## Codex

In a Codex space, **Codex** shows:

- **AUTHENTICATION**: your **ChatGPT Account** and whether it is **Connected**. When
  it isn't, **Connect your ChatGPT subscription** and **Connect** sign it in.
- **REMOTE CONTROL**: **Connected to ChatGPT**, with **Stop**.
- **PAIR A DEVICE**: pair your phone or computer to drive Codex in this space from
  the ChatGPT app. Choose **Pair a Device**.

![The Codex app in a Codex space's panel, highlighted: the ChatGPT Account not connected yet, with Connect, Remote Control connected to ChatGPT, and Pair a Device](/images/cloud/app-codex-pane.png)

See [Codex OAuth](/docs/cloud/setup/models/oauth/codex).
