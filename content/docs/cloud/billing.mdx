<Lead>
  **Billing** shows the plan you are on, when it renews and the payment methods
  on your account, and leads to the plans you can switch to.
</Lead>

## Open Billing

1. At the bottom of the sidebar, choose your account (your email address).
2. In the menu, choose **Billing**. The **Billing** panel opens: "Manage your
   subscription and payment methods".

## Your subscription

Under **Current Subscription**:

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | The plan you are on. |
| **Status** | Whether the subscription is **Active**. |
| **Billing Period** | The dates the current period covers. |
| **Renews on** | The date the plan renews. |

**Unsubscribe Plan** ends the subscription.

## Payment methods

**Payment Methods** lists the payment methods on your account. Choose **Add** to
add one.

## Compare plans

Choose **Check pricing plans** at the bottom of the panel. **Pricing** lists each
plan with its price, what it includes (for example how many spaces and how much
disk per space) and its support. Your plan is marked **Current Plan**; the others
have a **Join … Plan** button.

Plan names and prices are on the Pricing page in the app; they are not repeated
here so this page never shows an out-of-date price.
