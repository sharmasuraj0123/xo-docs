## A worked quarter

Three unit types (support tickets, contract reviews, weekly reports) in a
mid-size support-and-engineering operation. Every number computes from
[the calculus](/future-of-work/phase-1-agentic-workforce/unit-of-work-research/calculation);
parameters come from the micro-benchmark ranges of this research note. The table
is arithmetic, not measurement: its role is to exhibit the full calculation
chain, and the [validation program](/future-of-work/phase-1-agentic-workforce/unit-of-work-research/validation)'s
job is to replace it with production ledgers (experiment E5).

| June | 4,800 | 41,300 | 38,000 | $2,730 | $820 | $3,280 | $6,830 | 5.6x |

**Trend read:** QER 3.1 → 5.6 (+81%). Intervention rate 18.1% → 11.4%. Cost per
quirq 0.32 → 0.18. Quirq velocity +141%.

**The same quarter in tokens alone:** spend rose from $1,490 to $2,730, +83%, a
number indistinguishable from waste. That contrast is the whole argument: the
token bill says costs nearly doubled; the quirq ledger says verified value per
all-in dollar rose 81% while human rescue fell a third. One is an expense line,
the other is an investment case.

## The reading discipline

- **QER** answers *is the program paying*.
- **QER\* trend** (audit-corrected) answers *is it improving honestly*.
- **Intervention rate** answers *can it be trusted with more*, and its per-check
  decomposition names the next environment investment. In the worked quarter one
  support-ticket check (KB linking) drives most interventions; hardening that
  single check moves the whole company trajectory.
- **Cost per quirq, by unit type**, prices each category against its human
  baseline: a contract review at c_q = 0.13 against a paralegal-hour baseline is
  a decision, not a vibe.

## What adopting teams do first

No big bang required, and four habits carry all of it:

1. **Write definitions of done, not better prompts.** The leverage is in an end
   state the environment can check; prompt phrasing is a rounding error beside
   it. Start with one recurring task family, let the owner budget it. (How-to:
   the [playbook](/future-of-work/phase-1-agentic-workforce/unit-of-work-research/playbook).)
2. **Standardize the handoff package.** Every unit carries the same contract
   (done, budget, identity, state, record), so humans and agents exchange work
   under one process, not two.
3. **Run repeat work in the same environment.** The tenure curve of the
   [calculus](/future-of-work/phase-1-agentic-workforce/unit-of-work-research/calculation)
   only exists if memory and accepted definitions accumulate in one place;
   fresh contexts reset every unit to unit-1 economics.
4. **Read the ledger weekly; harden the worst check; repeat.** Audit
   environments the way you audit code: gameable checks, unmetered actions, and
   self-reported completion are bugs, and agents will find them.
