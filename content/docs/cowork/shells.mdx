<Lead>
  A shell is a terminal on the machine your Space runs on. Cowork keeps your
  shells in a dock under the main column; they keep running when you close their
  tab or move elsewhere.
</Lead>

Shells appear only when your Space has them turned on. Otherwise Cowork shows no
shell controls at all.

## Open a shell

- In a project: **Shell** beside **All threads** opens one in the project's folder.
- In the Workspace: **Shell** opens one in your projects folder.
- In a thread: **✎ → Open a shell in its worktree** opens one in the thread's
  own copy of the project.
- In the dock: **+ New shell in the workspace**.

Where shells already run, the button shows how many and opens their list.

## Use the Shells dock

**Shells** in the sidebar foot (or **Ctrl `**) shows or hides the dock. Its count
is the number of running shells. The dock holds your shells as tabs; **Every
shell** lists them all with their name, project, the program in front, whether
someone is watching or it is detached, and when a detached shell closes.

From the list you can **Open**, rename or close a shell, or **Close all
detached**. Closing a tab only detaches it: the shell keeps running, and a reload
reattaches it with its recent output. Cowork asks before closing a shell that is
running something.

![The Shells dock listing two detached shells, each closing in four hours, with Close all detached and New shell in the workspace](/images/cowork/shells.png)

Inside a shell every key goes to the shell, except **Ctrl `**. **Ctrl Shift C** and
**Ctrl Shift V** copy and paste.
