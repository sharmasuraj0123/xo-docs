<Lead>
  **Work** is where you look at what is in a space: its **Projects**, with what the
  agent did in each, and its **Files**, which you can browse and add to.
</Lead>

![Work in a space's panel: the Files and Projects cards, each with Open](/images/cloud/space-work.png)

**Work** has two cards: **Files** (browse, upload and manage files in your
workspace) and **Projects** (explore your space's structure). Choose **Open** on one.

## Projects

**Work › Projects** lists the space's projects, each with a short description and
**Open**.

![Work › Projects: three projects with their descriptions and Open buttons](/images/cloud/space-work-projects-pane.png)

Open a project to see:

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | The project's name, description, ID (**PID**), **OWNER** and when it was **CREATED**, plus its folder. |
| **Activity** | Whether a session is active ("No active sessions") and when it was last active. |
| **Stats** | **INPUT TOKENS**, **OUTPUT TOKENS**, **SESSIONS** and **FILES EDITED**, for 7 and 30 days. |
| **Todos** | How many todos are done, in progress and pending. |
| **Timeline** | How many events the project has recorded. |

![A project in the space: Identity, Activity, Stats and Todos](/images/cloud/space-project-pane.png)

For the full picture of a project, its files, Git history and sessions, open the
**Space** app (see [Apps](/docs/cloud/apps)): it is the
[XO Space UI](/docs/space/space-walk) for this space.

## Files

**Work › Files** shows the files in the space's workspace.

- **ADD FILES**: drop files on the box, or click it to browse. Up to 20 files at a
  time, of any type.
- **Filter names** narrows the list; **View** changes how it is shown; **Reload**
  reads the list again.
- **Open** opens a file.

![Work › Files with the upload box, the filter, View and Reload, and a list of files with Open](/images/cloud/space-work-files.png)
