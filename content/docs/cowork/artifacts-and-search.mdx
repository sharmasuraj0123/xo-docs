<Lead>
  Threads make documents, pictures and branches. **Artifacts** collects them, newest
  first, and **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on macOS) finds them, and any place, project or
  thread, by name.
</Lead>

## Browse Artifacts

1. Choose **Artifacts** in the sidebar. Its count is the number of things your
   threads made.
2. Narrow the list with **Find an artifact**, the project filter (**All
   projects**), or a kind: **All**, **Docs**, **Images** or **Branches**, each
   with its count.
3. Each card shows the title the agent gave it, its project, file name and age,
   and the thread that made it. A branch card shows its state, such as **merged ·
   PR #8** or **PR #17 open**.
4. Choose a file to open it in the viewer beside its own thread.

![Artifacts with eight items from today: documents, a launch image and branches](/images/cowork/artifacts.png)

Home's **Recently made** shows the newest ones.

## Search with Ctrl K

Press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on macOS) anywhere. With nothing typed, it lists **Home**,
**Workspace**, **Chats** and **Artifacts**, then each project and its threads with
their state. Type to match threads and made files by name, then choose a result
to go there.

![Ctrl K with release typed, matching two threads and one made file](/images/cowork/search.png)
