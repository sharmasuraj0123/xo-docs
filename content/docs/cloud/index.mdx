<Lead>
  **XO Cloud** at [app.xo.builders](https://app.xo.builders) runs your agents for
  you. Each **space** is a managed machine with one agent, the XO Space engine and
  its apps. You create spaces, open them from your browser, and follow what they

## XO Cloud or XO Space on your machine

Both run the same [XO Space](/docs/space) engine. Choose by where you want it to run.

| | **XO Space** on your machine | **XO Cloud** |
|---|---|---|
| **Runs on** | Your computer | A managed machine per space |
| **How you start** | [Install XO Space](/docs/space/install-space) | [Create an account](/docs/cloud/create-account), then **New Space** |
| **Agents** | The runtimes you have installed | Pick one when you create the space |
| **Where you work** | Space and Cowork in your browser, your own editor | The space's apps in your browser: Space, Cowork, Web VS Code, Terminal and more |
| **Teams** | Project sharing between Spaces | Organizations, and sharing a space with others |
