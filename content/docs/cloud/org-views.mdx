<Lead>
  In an organization, the chat can share the screen with **views**: the Issue
  Board, the organization's projects, and the Space and Cowork apps. Each view
  docks beside the chat, so you can follow the work while you talk to the agent.
</Lead>

## What you need

- Your organization open in **Org Spaces**. See
  [Switch to your organization](/docs/cloud/organizations#switch-to-your-organization).

## Open a view

1. In the bar along the top, choose the layout button at the far right, after
   **Connections**. It has no label; it shows a window icon.
2. Under **Views**, choose one: **Issue Board**, **Projects Management**,
   **Space**, **Cowork** or **Terminal**.

The view opens to the right of the chat, as a tab with its name. Open more and
they share that side of the screen, each with its own tab.

![The organization Argus with the layout menu open and highlighted: Views, with Issue Board, Projects Management, Space, Cowork, Terminal and Reset layout](/images/cloud/org-views-menu.png)

## Issue Board

**Issue Board** shows a project's issues in three rows: **Todo**, **In Progress**
and **Completed**, each with its count and arrows to scroll through its cards.
Each card shows the issue's number, its title and its labels; some carry a
**tracked** badge.

- Choose the project in the picker at the top right. The line under **Issue
  Board** says how many issues it has and how many are tracked.
- The refresh button beside the picker loads the issues again.

![The Issue Board view beside the chat, highlighted: Aurora Console's two issues under Todo, #42 tracked, and empty In Progress and Completed rows](/images/cloud/org-issue-board.png)

## Projects Management

**Projects Management** lists the organization's projects, with their count
("10 projects in this org space"). Each card shows the project's owner, its name
and description, who it is shared with (**Not shared** when nobody) and when it
was created.

![The Projects Management view beside the chat, highlighted: project cards for Aurora Console, Orbit API, Field Notes, Harbor Infrastructure and more, each with Alex Rivera as owner and Not shared](/images/cloud/org-projects.png)

### Open a project

Choose a project's name. It opens in a new tab of the same view, with:

- **Share**, at the top right.
- **Owner**, **Shared with** and **Created**.
- **FILES**: the project's files and folders, with a search box. The open file
  (**README.md** at first) shows beside the list, as **Preview** or **Code**, and
  is **Read only** here.

![A project opened from Projects Management, highlighted: Aurora Console with Share, Owner Alex Rivera, Shared with Not shared, Created 2 months ago, and README.md in Preview](/images/cloud/org-project.png)

## Space and Cowork

**Space** and **Cowork** open the organization's [XO Space](/docs/space/space-walk)
and [Cowork](/docs/agents/xo-cowork) apps in a view. Each has a reload button and
a button that opens the app in a new browser tab.

![The Space view beside the chat, highlighted: XO Space's Projects overview for the organization](/images/cloud/org-view-space.png)

![The Cowork view beside the chat, highlighted: Cowork's Home](/images/cloud/org-view-cowork.png)

## Terminal

**Terminal** opens a terminal in a view, like the other views.

## Arrange views

- A divider separates the chat from the views; drag it to give either more room.
- Each view has an expand button and **×** in its top right corner, and each tab
  has its own **×**.
- **Reset layout**, at the bottom of the layout menu, closes every view and
  leaves the chat on its own.

## Space apps and Connections

Two menus in the top bar sit next to the layout button:

- **Space apps** lists the organization's apps: **Space**, **Cowork** and
  **Terminal**.
- **Connections** lists the accounts the organization has connected, each with
  its state, for example **GitHub** and **Codex**, **Connected**.
