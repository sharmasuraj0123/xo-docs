<Lead>
  Connect your agent to **Telegram** or **Slack** so your team can talk to it from
  the tools they already use. You paste the channel's tokens on the space's **Setup ›
  Channels** page, then restart the OpenClaw Gateway.
</Lead>

<Callout type="info">
  Channels are for messaging agents such as OpenClaw. Coding agents such as Claude
  Code say "Channels not supported". See the
  [Agent Compatibility Guide](/docs/cloud/setup/compatibility).
</Callout>

## What you need

- A space whose status reads **Ready** (see [Your space](/docs/cloud/your-space)).
- **For Telegram:** a bot token from [@BotFather](https://t.me/botfather).
- **For Slack:** a Slack app with a Bot User OAuth Token (`xoxb-...`) and an
  App-Level Token (`xapp-...`). See the
  [Slack app deep-dive](/docs/agents/openclaw/channels/connect-slack) for creating the
  app and finding the tokens.

## Connect a channel

1. Open the space's **Setup** page and choose **Channels**. **Active Channels** shows
   how many channels are connected, for example **1/2**, and **NOTIFICATION
   CHANNELS** lists **Telegram** and **Slack** with their state.
2. Choose the channel's row. It opens with its token fields and **Save** and
   **Cancel**:
   - **Telegram:** **Bot Token**.
   - **Slack:** **Bot Token** (`xoxb-...`) and **App Token** (`xapp-...`).
3. Paste the tokens. They are hidden as you paste them.
4. Choose **Save**. XO Cloud confirms, for example **Slack saved**, and asks you to
   restart.

![Setup › Channels in an OpenClaw space with the Slack row open and highlighted: Bot Token and App Token pasted, Save and Cancel](/images/cloud/setup-channels-slack.png)

A connected channel's row reads **Connected**; open it to change the token or
choose **Disconnect**.

## Restart the OpenClaw Gateway

Channel changes take effect after the OpenClaw Gateway restarts.

1. Go back to the space's page: choose the space under **My Spaces** in the sidebar.
2. In the app grid, choose **OpenClaw Restart**. XO Cloud confirms with **OpenClaw
   gateway restarted**.

![An OpenClaw space's app grid with OpenClaw Restart highlighted, and the message OpenClaw gateway restarted](/images/cloud/setup-openclaw-restart.png)

<Callout type="warn">
  The message after **Save** says to restart from the **Launch** tab. Spaces no
  longer have that tab: use **OpenClaw Restart** in the app grid.
</Callout>

Send a test message to your agent on the channel you connected to check that it
answers.
