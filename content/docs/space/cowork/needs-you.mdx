<Lead>
  **Needs you** is a Cowork panel that shows one ask at a time, with the evidence beside it. An ask is a thread that is waiting on you. The panel opens beside the page and does not cover it. On a phone it opens as a bottom sheet.
</Lead>

## Open the panel

Any of these opens it:

- press **N**;
- click the button on **Your next move** on [Home](/docs/space/cowork/home), a **Then** row, or **+N more**;
- click **◆ N worth your time** on the status line above the composer;
- click **Next move** in the [Workspace](/docs/space/cowork/workspace);
- click **Decide** on a thread card in a project chat.

Inside a thread that is itself waiting on you, the ask appears as a card above the composer instead. That docked card works the same way as the panel.

## Read an ask

The header reads **Needs you** with a position such as **2 of 5**. Each card shows:

- the effort label;
- `project › thread title`;
- the headline;
- one line of detail;
- the number of checklist steps left.

**Details** (press **E**) expands, in place:

- the agent's full message;
- the last two messages in the thread;
- the checklist as **Checklist x of y**;
- the thread's branch.

Use **‹** / **›** or **K** / **J** to move between asks. Skipping is always allowed: *Skip freely; nothing here is forced first.*

## Answer an ask in Live

| Ask type | Buttons | What Cowork posts to the thread |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A question (needs a reply) | **Reply in words**, which opens **Your answer, in your words**, then **Send** | Your text |
| An approval (yes/no) | **Allow** · **Send back**. Send back asks *What should it do instead? (optional)* | `Approved.` or `Not approved. {your note}` |
| A stopped thread | **Try again** · **Tell it what to try** | `Please try again from the failing step.` or your text |

Every answer becomes an ordinary text reply in that thread. The thread's agent reads it and continues. **Allow** does not grant any tool, file, or sandbox permission by itself. It only sends the word `Approved.` to the agent.

An ask is an approval only when the Space API marks the thread as needing approval. Every other waiting thread is a question.

## Undo

After you answer, Cowork waits **3 seconds** before posting. A toast such as **Answered** `{ask}` · **Undo** counts down. Click **Undo** or press **U** to cancel. The toast then reads *Undone. Nothing was sent; the ask is back.*

If posting fails, the ask returns to your list with *Didn't reach the agent: `{title}`. It's back in your list.*

## When the list is empty

The panel ends on **That's everything.** with a summary such as *1 answered · 1 sent back*. It closes on its own after about 2 seconds, and the status line shows **All clear**.

After about 25 minutes of answering asks, the panel offers a pause with **Take a break** or **Keep going**.

## Not available in Live

These panel features exist only in Demo mode, because the Space API does not yet send risk levels, options, or commands with an ask:

- **Hold to allow** for destructive asks;
- **Allow all N routine**;
- **Make it a rule**;
- **Later today**;
- numbered answer options **1–9**;
- the coordinator's suggested answer.

See [Live and Demo](/docs/space/cowork/live-and-demo).
