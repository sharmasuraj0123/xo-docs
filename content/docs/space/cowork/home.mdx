<Lead>
  **Home** is the page Cowork opens on. It answers one question: what needs you right now, across every project? Open it with **Home** in the sidebar or press **H**.
</Lead>

![Cowork Home in Demo mode with six things worth your time, the next-move card for a mobile-app ask, the Then list, and Found while working](/images/space/cowork/home.png)

*Demo mode. The answer buttons on the card (**Dim to 38%**, **Muted grey**, **Show me both**), the **Suggests** line, the **Glance** effort label, and **Found while working** appear only in Demo.*

Cowork has no page URLs. Reloading the browser always returns to Home, and the browser's Back button does not move between Cowork screens. Use **Esc**, the sidebar, or [Jump (⌘K)](/docs/space/cowork/keyboard) instead.

## What Home shows

From top to bottom:

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Head** | The date, a greeting, one summary sentence, and a **Create XO Project** button |
| **Your next move** | One card: the ask that is most worth your time now |
| **Then** | Up to two more asks, then **+N more** |
| **Handled** | One line counting running work. Click it to expand. |

The summary sentence is one of:

- **One thing is worth your time.**
- **Three things are worth your time.** (the number is spelled out up to ten)
- **Nothing needs you.**
- **XO can't be reached right now.** This appears when the Space API is unreachable and nothing has loaded yet.

The sidebar's **Home** row shows an amber ◆ and the number of asks. The browser tab title shows the same count (`◆ N`).

## What counts as "needs you"

In Live mode, a thread becomes an **ask** when its state is one of these:

| Thread state | What the card offers | Card button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Needs a reply | A question from the thread's agent. You answer in words. | **Read and reply** |
| Needs approval | A yes/no request. | **Decide** |
| Stopped (API state `failed`) | The thread stopped at a step. | **Look and choose** |

The ask's title is the first sentence of the agent's last message, up to 140 characters. If the agent left no message, the title is **It's waiting for your reply**. Done and resolved threads never produce asks.

## Your next move

The card shows:

- an effort label: **Decide ~1 min** for every Live ask;
- where the ask comes from, as `project › thread title`;
- the headline, which is the ask in one line;
- an estimate in the form **N steps left in its checklist**;
- one button that opens the ask in the [Needs you panel](/docs/space/cowork/needs-you).

Asks are ranked so that the ones unblocking the most remaining checklist work come first. If the same ask is already open in the panel, the card reads **Open in the panel**.

## Handled

The bottom line reads **Handled · three working** or **Handled · nothing running**. Click it to list every busy thread with its project, title, current step, and a small progress bar. Threads in this list do not need you.

## Empty and offline states

| Text | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Nothing needs you.** 4 threads working. They'll ask when it's worth it. | Agents are busy, and none is waiting on you. |
| **Nothing needs you.** Start something below, or enjoy the quiet. | Nothing is running and nothing is waiting. |
| **Can't reach XO.** Retrying every few seconds. Nothing you write is lost; sending waits until it's back. | Cowork cannot reach the Space API. |

The composer at the bottom of Home sends to the [Workspace](/docs/space/cowork/workspace) coordinator unless you aim it with `@project`. See [Composer](/docs/space/cowork/composer).
