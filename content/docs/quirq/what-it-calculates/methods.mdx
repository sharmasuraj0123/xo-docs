<Lead>Every claim is tested on an open, dependency-free reference harness, under a discipline that separates what the machinery proves from what only real agents can prove.</Lead>

## The harness

Snapshots, weighted checks, the mint and settlement rules, a hash-chained
ledger written by the environment, mock agents, and a runner for a production
coding agent. Specs are pre-registered: hypothesis, method, metric, and
falsifier stated before results. The results ledger is content-addressed, so a
skeptic re-runs and compares hashes rather than trusting prose.
