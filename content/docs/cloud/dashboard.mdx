<Lead>
  After you sign in, XO Cloud opens on the **Dashboard**: a usage summary and every
  space you can open. The sidebar on the left is the same on every page.
</Lead>

![The XO Cloud dashboard with the sidebar, Welcome back, the usage summary and the Spaces table](/images/cloud/home.png)

## The sidebar

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Scope switcher** (top) | Switch between **Personal Spaces** (your own workspaces) and **Org Spaces** (shared with your organization). |
| **Agents** | **New Space** creates a space. **Dashboard** and **Usage** open those pages. |
| **My Spaces** | One row per space you own. Choose one to open it. Its **…** menu has **Copy Space ID** and **Delete Space**. |
| **Spaces Shared with me** | Spaces other people shared with you. |
| **Resources** | **GitHub**, **Docs** and **Report a Bug**. |
| Bottom | The theme switch (**Dark**) and your account. |

![The scope switcher open: Personal Spaces, checked, and Org Spaces](/images/cloud/sidebar-scope-menu.png)

![A space's menu in My Spaces: Space Action, with Copy Space ID and Delete Space](/images/cloud/sidebar-space-menu.png)

The bar along the top shows where you are, for example **Spaces › atlas-research
› Setup**, and the agent type of the space beside its name.

## The dashboard

- **Welcome back** with **New Space** at the right.
- **Usage Summary**: **Cost**, **Messages** and **Tokens** for the chosen period
  (**Last 7 days** by default), with a daily bar chart of cost and one of tokens.
  The [Usage page](/docs/cloud/usage) has more detail.
- **Spaces**: your spaces and the spaces shared with you.

## The Spaces table

Type in **Filter spaces...** to find a space by name, or filter by **Role** (for
example **Personal**) or **Type** (the agent: Claude-Code, Xo-Cowork, Openclaw,
Hermes, Codex). Each row shows the space's **Status**, **Running** or **Stopped**,
with **Copy ID** and **Delete**. To open a space, choose it under **My Spaces** in the sidebar.

![The Spaces table: five spaces with their role, agent type, status, Copy ID and Delete](/images/cloud/home-spaces-table.png)
