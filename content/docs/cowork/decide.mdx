<Lead>
  When an agent needs you, Cowork puts it on **Home** and in the **Needs you**
  panel. You handle one decision at a time, in any order, and the agent goes on
  with your answer.
</Lead>

## Read Home

**Home** is the first item in the sidebar. Its count is the number of asks
waiting. From top to bottom it shows:

- The date, a greeting and how many things are worth your time, for example
  "Four things are worth your time." **Create XO Project** is at the top right.
- **Your next move**: the most useful decision to make now. The card says what
  kind of ask it is and roughly how long it takes ("Decide ~1 min"), which project
  and thread it comes from, its title, and how many steps are left in that
  thread's checklist. **Decide** opens it.
- **Then**: the other asks, one row each, with their project. **+1 more** (or
  however many) shows the rest.
- **Handled**: folded by default. It lists what is running without you, with
  each thread's current step.
- **Recently made**: the newest files your threads made.
- A composer at the bottom that writes to the **Workspace** coordinator (see
  [Give work to a coordinator](/docs/cowork/coordinators)).

![Handled opened: two working threads, each with its project and current step](/images/cowork/home-handled.png)

## Open the Needs you panel

Choose **Decide** on Home, on a row under **Then**, or on a thread card. The
**Needs you** panel opens on the right with the first decision. Its header says
which one you are on ("Needs you 1 of 4"), and the progress marks show how many
are left.

![Home with the Needs you panel open beside it on the first of four decisions](/images/cowork/needs-you.png)

Every card shows:

- What kind of ask it is and an estimate of the time it takes.
- **project › thread**. Choose it (or press **O**) to open the thread beside the panel.
- The title and the agent's reason for asking.
- How many steps are left in the thread's checklist.
- **Details** (**E**): the full request, for example the exact command and why
  the agent wants it, and the thread's branch.

At the bottom, **‹** (**K**) and **›** (**J**) move between decisions. "Skip
freely; nothing here is forced first." You can leave a decision and come back.
**Close** (**Esc**) puts the panel away.

## Answer a permission request

An agent asks before it does something it may not do on its own, such as
installing a package.

1. Read the title and, if you need to, open **Details** to see the exact command.
2. Choose **Allow once** (**A**) to let it run this time, or **Deny** (**D**).
3. To stop being asked about the same thing, turn on **Make it a rule** (**T**)
   first. The main button becomes **Allow for this thread**, and Cowork lists the
   rules you can make, for example **Always allow `npm install csv-stringify@6`
   in Aurora Console**, or the same permission for this thread only. Choose one.

![A permission request with Make it a rule turned on, showing Allow for this thread and an Always allow rule for the project](/images/cowork/decision-permission.png)

Rules you make appear in [Settings › Rules](/docs/cowork/settings#rules), where you can revoke them.

## Answer a question

When an agent needs a choice, the card lists the answers it thinks fit, each with
a short description. Choose one (or press its number, **1**–**9**), or choose
**Answer in words** to write your own answer.

![A question card asking how sync conflicts should resolve, with three answers and Answer in words](/images/cowork/decision-question.png)

## Approve or send back

When a thread has something ready for your sign-off, such as a draft, the card
says what is ready. Choose **Approve**, or **Send back** to return it to the agent.

![An approval card for a launch announcement with Approve and Send back](/images/cowork/decision-approval.png)

## Reply to a waiting thread

Some threads are simply waiting for a reply. Their card offers **Reply in words**,
**Done** and **Open the thread**.

## Keyboard

| Key | On a decision |
|---|---|
| **A** | Allow once |
| **D** | Deny |
| **T** | Turn **Make it a rule** on or off |
| **1**–**9** | Choose an answer |
| **E** | Details |
| **O** | Open the thread |
| **J** / **K** | Next / previous decision |
| **Esc** | Close the panel |

Settings › **Keyboard** lists every shortcut and lets you change them.
