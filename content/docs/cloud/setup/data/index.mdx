Data sources let your agent read from external accounts. Credentials live in the space's workspace, not on the XO platform.

Open the space's **Setup** page and choose **Data**. **DATA SOURCES** lists **Google Drive**, **OneDrive**, **GitHub** and **Vercel**, each with **Connect**, or **Connected** and **Disconnect** once it is connected.
