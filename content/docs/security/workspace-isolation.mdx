When you launch an agent, it runs inside its own **isolated cloud workspace** — a dedicated, sandboxed environment with its own compute and its own private storage. This page explains what that isolation means and how it protects you.

---

## Your workspace is a sandbox

Each project runs in a separate environment that is walled off from:

- **Other users.** No other customer's workspace can see, reach, or interfere with yours. Each workspace is isolated from every other tenant on the platform.
- **Your local machine.** Your agent works entirely in the cloud. It never touches your laptop, your files, or your local network unless you explicitly connect to it.
- **The rest of the platform.** A workspace only has access to the resources assigned to it — not to internal systems or other services.

<Callout type="info">
	Because everything runs remotely, you can safely let an agent install
	dependencies, run code, and modify files without any risk to your own device.
</Callout>

---

## Hardened by design

Isolation is only half the story — the environment itself is built to reduce risk:

- **Runs unprivileged.** Workspace processes run as a normal, non-root user rather than with administrative rights, so a compromised process has far less it can do.
- **Minimal, purpose-built images.** Environments are based on lean, hardened base images that ship only what's needed — fewer components means a smaller attack surface.
- **Automatically patched.** The underlying operating system and platform receive security updates automatically. You're never left running unpatched infrastructure.

---

## Protected on the network

Traffic to and from your workspace is guarded at several layers:

- **Encrypted connections.** All access to your workspace and its tools goes over HTTPS/TLS. Plain, unencrypted requests are redirected to secure ones.
- **Managed Web Application Firewall.** A managed WAF sits in front of the platform and blocks common web attacks — including SQL injection, cross-site scripting (XSS), remote code execution attempts, and automated vulnerability scanners — before they reach any workspace.
- **Abuse and rate limiting.** Excessive or abusive traffic is automatically throttled, which helps absorb bursts and denial-of-service attempts.
- **Private internal networking.** Supporting systems such as databases are not exposed to the public internet; they're reachable only over private, internal networking.

---

## Restricted administrative access

Access to the infrastructure that runs your workspaces is tightly controlled:

- Only a small set of authorized team members can administer the platform.
- Administrative access requires authenticated identity, and those actions are logged and auditable.
- There are no shared passwords or long-lived keys handed around for infrastructure access.

---

## In short

Your workspace is a private, sandboxed, encrypted environment that other users can't reach, that runs hardened and patched by default, and that sits behind a managed firewall. Combined with how we handle your data, that's what "secure by default" means at XO.
