<Lead>
  You can share a space with someone who has an XO Cloud account. They find it
  under **Spaces Shared with me** in their sidebar.
</Lead>

## What you need

- A space of your own. See [Create a space](/docs/cloud/create-a-space).
- The exact email address the other person uses for XO Cloud. They need an
  account first; see [Create an account](/docs/cloud/create-account).

## Share a space

1. Open the space from **My Spaces** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Share Space** at the top right.
3. In the **Share Space** dialog, type the person's exact email address in the
   search box ("Search by exact email address...").
4. When XO Cloud finds them, give them access from the result.

If no XO Cloud account uses that address, the dialog says "No user found with that
email address." Check the spelling, or ask them to create an account first.

![The Share Space dialog with an email address typed and No user found with that email address](/images/cloud/share-space-dialog.png)

Spaces shared with you appear under **Spaces Shared with me** in the sidebar
("No shared spaces yet" until someone shares one), and in the dashboard's
**Spaces** table.
