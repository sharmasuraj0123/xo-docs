Work is moving from people using tools to people directing agents. This section
consolidates how we at XO think about that shift, as one continuous argument you
can read start to finish, alongside independent takes we keep returning to.

## The story in five sentences

Software is becoming a worker rather than a tool, and the constraint is no
longer capability but trust at scale. Trust needs a contract: the **unit of
work**, an outcome with a definition of done, a budget, and a single owner,
living in a workspace rather than a prompt. A contract needs a unit of account:
the **quirq**, minted when verification confirms delivery against the budget a
human set, so AI's business impact finally gets a meter the way tokens meter its
energy. Once many parties share that contract, humans stay the brain and agents
become the runtime, and the org becomes part human, part agent, settling into
one blended ledger. Eventually the systems begin improving themselves, and what
remains human is direction, judgment, and guardrails.
