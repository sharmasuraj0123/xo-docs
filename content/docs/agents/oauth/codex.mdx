Connect your OpenAI Codex subscription to an XO agent via OAuth — no API key required. This flow is shared across any agent that exposes a **Connect Codex** button (OpenClaw, future agents).

## What You'll Need

- An XO project that has finished provisioning
- An OpenAI account with a Codex subscription

## Connect Your Codex Account

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Your Project
    Open your project on [beta.xo.builders](https://beta.xo.builders).

    ![Project page](/images/codex-oauth-01-project-page.jpeg)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Click Connect Codex
    Next to the **Configure** button on your project card, click **Connect Codex**.

    ![Click Connect Codex](/images/codex-oauth-02-connect-codex.jpeg)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Open the Authorization Page
    In the Connect Codex modal, click **Open authorization page**. A new browser tab opens with the OpenAI sign-in.

    ![Click Open authorization page](/images/codex-oauth-03-open-auth-page.jpeg)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sign In to OpenAI
    Choose **Continue with Google**, or sign in with your preferred method.

    ![Continue with Google](/images/codex-oauth-04-continue-with-google.jpeg)

    If you have multiple Google accounts, click **Use another account** and choose the one tied to your Codex subscription.

    ![Use another account](/images/codex-oauth-05-use-another-account.jpeg)

    Enter your email address and press **Enter**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Verify Your Identity
    OpenAI may ask you to verify your identity. Choose your preferred verification method.

    ![Choose a verification method](/images/codex-oauth-07-verify-identity.jpeg)

    Select **Email** to receive a verification code by email.

    ![Select Email verification](/images/codex-oauth-08-email-method.jpeg)

    Enter the code OpenAI sends you and click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Authorize Codex
    Review the permissions and click **Continue** to authorize the connection.

    ![Click Continue to authorize](/images/codex-oauth-10-authorize.jpeg)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Confirm and Close
    If a connection error appears, click **Try again**.

    ![Click Try again](/images/codex-oauth-11-try-again.jpeg)

    Once the connection is confirmed, close the setup modal.

    ![Close the modal](/images/codex-oauth-12-confirm-close.jpeg)
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your Codex subscription is now connected. Your agent will use it for all model calls.
