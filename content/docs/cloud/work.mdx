<Lead>
  **Work** is where you look at what is in a space: its **Projects**, with what the
  agent did in each, and its **Files**, which you can browse and add to.
</Lead>

![A space's page with Work open in its panel, highlighted: the Files and Projects cards, each with Open](/images/cloud/space-work.png)

**Work** has two cards: **Files** (browse, upload and manage files in your
workspace) and **Projects** (visualize and explore your space's structure). Choose
**Open** on one.

## Projects

**Work › Projects** lists the space's projects, each with a short description and
**Open**.

![Work › Projects in the space's panel, highlighted: Field Notes, Latency Study and Retrieval Lab, each with a description and Open](/images/cloud/space-work-projects-pane.png)

For the full picture of a project, its files, Git history and sessions, open the
**Space** app (see [Apps](/docs/cloud/apps)): it is the
[XO Space UI](/docs/space/space-walk) for this space.

## Files

**Work › Files** shows the files in the space's workspace.

- **ADD FILES**: drop files on the box, or click it to browse. Up to 20 files at a
  time, of any type.
- **Filter names** narrows the list; **View** changes how it is shown; **Reload**
  reads the list again.
- **Open** opens a file.

![Work › Files in the space's panel, highlighted: the ADD FILES box, the filter, View and Reload, and five files with Open](/images/cloud/space-work-files.png)
