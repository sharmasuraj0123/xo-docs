This is the running weekly record of changes shipped across XO. The page footer shows the date this page was last built.

## November 25, 2026

### ☁️ Managed Cloud section rebuilt

- The section now follows XO Cloud itself: **Get started** ([create an account](/docs/cloud/create-account), [create a space](/docs/cloud/create-a-space), [find your way around](/docs/cloud/dashboard)), **Your space** ([your space](/docs/cloud/your-space), [apps](/docs/cloud/apps), [Browser VS Code](/docs/cloud/vs-code-server), [Work](/docs/cloud/work), [Setup](/docs/cloud/setup), [Secrets](/docs/cloud/secrets), [Usage](/docs/cloud/usage)) and **Teams and account** ([share a space](/docs/cloud/share-project), [organizations](/docs/cloud/organizations), [billing](/docs/cloud/billing)).
- **Setup** (models, data sources, channels) moved into Managed Cloud, since it is a page of a cloud space. Old links redirect.
- New pages: [Your space](/docs/cloud/your-space), [Work](/docs/cloud/work), [Secrets](/docs/cloud/secrets), [Apps](/docs/cloud/apps), [Usage](/docs/cloud/usage), [Organizations](/docs/cloud/organizations), starting with switching to **Org Spaces**, and [Billing](/docs/cloud/billing).
- Every Managed Cloud screenshot is new: a full app window at 2560 × 1600, with the step's subject highlighted. Screenshots across the docs are now served at full resolution, so their text stays sharp; click an image to see it full size.

## August 27, 2026

### 🚀 New features & improvements

- **Resizable feature on projects page** — interactively adjust panes and split views to customize your workspace layout.
- **Improved UI for tablet and mobile aspect ratios** — responsive layouts and views optimized for touch and compact screens.
- **Rich text editor for bug reporting** — reporting bugs now supports rich text editing along with images for us to resolve your issues faster.
