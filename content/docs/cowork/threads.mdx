<Lead>
  A thread is one task given to one agent. It has its own checklist, its own
  branch and its own copy of the project to work in. Open one from a thread card's
  **Open →**, a **Now** chip, **All threads**, or **Ctrl K**.
</Lead>

![A working thread with its checklist open at step 2 of 4, the conversation, changed-file chips and the composer](/images/cowork/thread.png)

## Read the header

- **‹ project** (or **Esc**) goes back to the project.
- The title, then the thread's state: **Working**, **Needs you**, **Idle** and so
  on. "Working" means its agent is busy right now.
- The runtime it runs on, such as **Claude Code**, when your workspace has more
  than one runtime.
- **Resolve** marks it resolved: nothing more is owed.
- **✎** ("Rename, label and more") opens **Rename**, **Add a label**, **Open a
  shell in its worktree** and **Continue on another runtime…**.
- The last button opens the panel with the thread's changes, agents and Map (see
  [Review changes and files](/docs/cowork/changes-and-files)).

## Read the checklist

Under the header, the checklist bar shows progress ("2 of 4"), the current step
and the thread's branch. Choose it to see every step: **✓** done, **✱** the
current step, **○** still to do. When the thread waits for you, the bar reads
"Paused on you".

## Read the conversation

Your messages are on the right; the agent's are on the left. Between them, Cowork
summarises the work instead of showing every tool call:

- "Worked 2m 51s · 3 steps": choose it to unfold the steps.
- An activity line, such as "Created 1 file, edited 2 files, ran 1 command".
- One chip per changed file, with its added and removed lines. Choose a chip to
  see its changes in the panel; **Ctrl**-click (**⌘**-click on macOS) opens the
  file in Files.
- While the agent works, a live row shows its current step and for how long.

![File chips for three changed files with their added and removed line counts](/images/cowork/changed-files.png)

## Answer what the thread needs

A thread that needs you shows its decision above the composer, the same card as
in the Needs you panel, with **Read the thread first** to fold it while you read.
See [Decide what needs you](/docs/cowork/decide).

![A thread paused on you, with a permission request above the composer](/images/cowork/thread-decision.png)

## Reply to the agent

Type in the composer ("Reply to the agent…"). The line under it says "Reply in
**this thread**": the reply goes straight to this thread's agent, not to a
coordinator. While the agent works, the round button is **Stop**: it stops the
agent, which keeps what is already done.

![The thread composer with Reply in this thread, the Sonnet · Balanced model chip and the stop button](/images/cowork/composer-thread.png)

## Choose the model, thinking and runtime

The model chip (for example **Sonnet · Balanced**) opens **Intelligence for this
thread**, or press **Ctrl .**:

- **Model**: **Opus** (for complex work), **Sonnet** (fast and capable) or
  **Haiku** (quick answers).
- **Thinking**: **Quick**, **Balanced** or **Deep**. Balanced is "a sensible
  default for most work."
- **Runtime**: the runtime the thread runs on.
- **Default model** goes back to the default.

Changes apply from the next turn.

![Intelligence for this thread: Opus, Sonnet and Haiku, the Quick, Balanced and Deep thinking choices and the runtime](/images/cowork/model-picker.png)

A thread keeps one runtime for its whole life. Choosing another runtime, here or
with **Continue on another runtime…**, continues the work in a new thread on that
runtime. The two threads link to each other, and the old one reads "Continued".
