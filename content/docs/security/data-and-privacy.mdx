Your code, files, and credentials are yours. This page explains how XO protects them — from encryption to durable storage to who can access what.

---

## Encryption in transit and at rest

- **In transit.** Every connection to XO — the dashboard, your IDE, your agent's tools — is encrypted with TLS. Insecure requests are automatically upgraded to secure ones.
- **At rest.** The disks that hold your workspace files and the databases behind the platform are encrypted at rest by default, so your data is protected even at the storage layer.

---

## Your files stay put

Workspaces are designed so your work is never lost to routine operations:

- **Persistent storage.** Each workspace has its own persistent disk. Your files survive stopping and restarting your machine.
- **Preserved across instance changes.** If your workspace moves between instance types — for example, when you change plans — your files and data move with it. The disk is preserved, not recreated.
- **Backed up.** Platform data is backed up so it can be recovered if something goes wrong.

<Callout type="info">
	Stopping your machine to save resources is safe — your files are waiting for
	you when you start it again. See
	[Manage Your Machine](/getting-started/manage-workspace).
</Callout>

## How we handle secrets

API keys, tokens, and other credentials are treated as sensitive throughout the platform:

- **Stored in a managed vault.** Secrets live in a dedicated, managed secrets store — never hard-coded into applications or committed to source repositories.
- **Least privilege.** Each service can read only the specific secrets it needs, with read-only access. Nothing has blanket access to everything.
- **Keyless internal authentication.** Internal services authenticate to one another using short-lived, identity-based credentials rather than long-lived keys that could leak.
- **Rotatable.** Credentials can be rotated without downtime, so a key can be replaced quickly if it ever needs to be.

---

## Access is under your control

- **Authenticated accounts.** Access to your workspaces requires a signed-in XO account.
- **Project-scoped access.** A project is visible only to you and the people you explicitly invite.
- **You manage sharing.** You decide who collaborates on a project, and you can revoke access at any time. See [Share Your Project](/getting-started/share-project).

---

## Built on trusted infrastructure

XO runs on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with certified data-center, hardware, and physical-security protections. On top of that foundation, we add the workspace isolation, network filtering, encryption, and access controls described across this section — so security is layered from the physical facility all the way up to your agent.
