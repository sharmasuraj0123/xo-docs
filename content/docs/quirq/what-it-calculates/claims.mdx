## Limitations

The budget is the model's entry point for human error: a mispriced outcome
mints mispriced quirqs, and budget governance is institutional, not mechanical.
Definitions of done are incomplete contracts; work whose acceptance is
irreducibly judgmental enters the ledger only through the owner's verdict,
which restores a human bottleneck exactly where the work is fuzziest. The
energy bridge inherits the wide measured variance of per-token energy. The
measured tier is mock mode: it validates machinery, not agents. And a unit of
account reshapes the behavior of what it measures; the mitigations are
designed and exhibited in miniature, and unproven at scale, which is why the
validation program, not the paper, is the product.
