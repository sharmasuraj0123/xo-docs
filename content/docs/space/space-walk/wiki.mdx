<Lead>
  **Wiki** is the operating manual bundled with Space. Open the fifth tab, press **5**, or use `/space/#/wiki`. Its articles are served with the app and can be read without an external documentation service.
</Lead>

![The built-in Wiki with its article navigation and storage guide](/images/space/wiki.png)

## Find a guide

Choose an article in the left navigation. It includes installation and first-run help, watcher behavior, storage catalogs, flow design, collaboration, and guides for the application views.

- Start with **Storage & data map** to understand the distinction between project files, portable `.xo` metadata, machine-local state, and native runtime stores.
- Read **How the watcher works** when a project or session seems absent.
- Choose a **Tab guide** for a view's controls, API sources, and troubleshooting. Use its action at the end, such as **Open Projects** or **Open Sessions**, to return to the live view.
- Use **Everything in .xo** and **Everything in .quirq** as storage references when integrating with Space.

Switching away and back preserves the selected article and its scroll position during that page session. Selecting another article starts it at the top. The URL identifies the Wiki tab; article selection is held in the current browser page.

## Match guidance to the running app

Wiki is versioned application code. Updating the documentation website does not change the Wiki inside an older installation, and reading an API example there does not execute it. After updating Space, reload the browser to receive its bundled guides.

Use [Setup](/docs/space/space-walk/setup) for the roots and runtime configured on your machine, and use the relevant live view for its current data and freshness timestamps. Default paths in an article are examples, not proof of your installation's configuration.

The Wiki also discusses **Space Walk session replay**, a separate tool. That article does not add a replay tab to this UI; see [Session replay](/docs/space/space-walk/replay).
