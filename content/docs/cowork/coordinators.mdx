<Lead>
  You don't start agents one by one. You tell a coordinator what should get done:
  the **Workspace** coordinator for anything across your projects, or a project's
  own coordinator. It answers, forwards your message, or starts a thread for the
  work.
</Lead>

## Write to the Workspace

1. Choose **Workspace** in the sidebar. Its header counts what is working and
   what finished today across your projects.
2. Type what should get done in the composer ("Ask anything across your
   projects…") and send it.
3. To aim a message at one project, start with `@` and the project's name, for
   example `@aurora-console`. The line under the composer shows where the message
   goes: "To the **Workspace** coordinator · @ to aim at a project".

A message the Workspace passes on shows a receipt such as **Routed to
aurora-console**. In that project's conversation, the same message reads
"Forwarded by the **Workspace**". Asks that come back wait on Home, not in the
conversation.

![The Workspace conversation, with a message routed to aurora-console and the coordinator's reply](/images/cowork/workspace.png)

The composer on Home writes to the Workspace in the same way.

![The Workspace composer with its placeholder, the routing line and Auto](/images/cowork/composer-workspace.png)

## Work in a project

Choose a project in the sidebar (or type in **Find a project**). Its page is the
conversation with the project's coordinator:

- The header shows how many threads are working and done, and the **Now** shelf:
  one chip per thread in progress, each with a progress bar.
- In the conversation, every thread the coordinator started appears as a card
  with its title, its state ("Needs you · ~1 min", "Working · 2 of 4",
  "Done · 3 of 3"), its current step or ask, its branch, and **Open →**. A thread
  that needs you also has **Decide**.
- The composer writes "To the aurora-console coordinator" (with your project's name).

![A project's conversation with thread cards and the Now shelf of threads in progress](/images/cowork/project.png)

![A thread card that needs you, with its ask, branch, Open and Decide](/images/cowork/thread-card.png)

### See every thread

**Threads** in the project header opens **All threads** for the project. Search by
title, or filter by state: **All**, **Needs you**, **Working**, **Waiting**,
**Done** and **Stopped**, each with its count. Finished threads are folded at the
bottom.

![All threads for aurora-console, filtered to All, with two that need you and one working](/images/cowork/project-threads.png)

## Choose the coordinator's model

The chip on the right of the composer (**Auto** by default) opens **Intelligence**
for the coordinator. Choose **Auto** to let the coordinator pick, or a model
(Opus, Sonnet or Haiku). The choice applies from the next turn. Threads have their
own picker: see [Follow a thread](/docs/cowork/threads#choose-the-model-thinking-and-runtime).

## Create a project

Choose **Create XO Project** at the top right of Home, or **+ New** beside
**Projects** in the sidebar. A new project gets its own coordinator, plan and
memory.

- **Start empty**: enter a **Project name** and, optionally, **What is it for?**
  Cowork shows the folder it will create ("Saved as …/harbor-status-page"). Choose
  **Create project**.
- **From GitHub**: enter the **Repository** as `owner/repo` or a Git URL, then the
  name and description, and choose **Clone project**. Cowork shows the GitHub
  account it will use ("Connected as …"); private repositories work too.
