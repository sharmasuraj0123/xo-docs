<Lead>
  The **Workspace** is your coordinator across all projects. Each **project** has its own coordinator, which answers you or starts threads to do the work. Both views are conversations, with the work they started shown inline.
</Lead>

For how a message moves from the Workspace to a project and into a thread, see [How messages travel](/docs/agents/xo-cowork/how-messages-travel).

## The Workspace

The header shows the XO mark, **Workspace**, and "Your coordinator, across all N projects." Below it is a row of counts:

| Count | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Working** | Threads whose agent is busy. |
| **Need you** (amber) | Threads waiting on you. |
| **Can wait** | Small asks held for later. |
| **Idle** | Open threads with nothing running right now (shown only when there are any). |
| **Finished today** (green) | Threads finished today. |

Then either a **Next move** button (how long it takes, and its title), which opens that decision, or "Nothing needs you. Ask anything below."

### Messages

- **Your messages** can show where they went: "Routed to **billing-api**" (click it to open the project) or "Forwarded by the **Workspace**".
- **Coordinator messages** are labelled **Coordinator** with the time.
- **Thread cards** appear in the conversation when work starts.
- An empty Workspace reads "No messages yet. Ask the coordinator anything below."
- Scroll to the top to load **Earlier messages**. You see "Loading earlier messages…" while it loads, and "Beginning of the conversation" when there is nothing older.

<Callout type="info" title="Demo only">
  In Demo mode the Workspace also shows "N rules answered N asks for you".
</Callout>

## A project

Open a project from the sidebar. The header shows:

- The project name.
- A summary: "N need(s) you · N working · N done this week · N resolved", "All quiet", or "No threads yet".
- The description you gave when you created it (shown only until you close the browser tab).
- An **All threads** button with a count.

### The Now strip

Under the header, the **Now** strip holds one slim chip for each thread in progress, in this order: needs you, then stopped, then working. Each chip shows a state mark, the title and progress. The strip scrolls sideways, and your mouse wheel scrolls it too.

### The conversation

A project's conversation is your exchange with its coordinator, with thread cards in between. Short lines record what happened:

- "X finished" and "X stopped".
- Your decisions, such as "You allowed · …".
- "Not sent · … · why", when a decision could not reach the agent.

An empty project reads "No messages yet. Tell this project's coordinator what you'd like done."

### Thread cards

A thread card shows:

- The title.
- Where it stands: "Needs you · ~1 min", "Working · 2 of 4", "Done · 3 of 3", or **Idle**, **Paused**, **Stopped** or **Resolved**.
- Its checklist as progress segments.
- What it's doing: "◆ ask" when it waits on you, "✱ current step" while it works, or why it stopped.
- The branch it works on, "a small ask can wait" when one is held back, and how long ago it last moved.
- **Open →**, plus a **Decide** button when it needs you.

### All threads

**All threads** opens a sheet titled "All threads · project":

- **Search threads** matches a title or branch.
- Filters: **All**, **Needs you**, **Working**, **Done**, **Stopped**, each with a count. A filter with nothing in it is greyed out.
- Each row shows the state, title, label, progress and age.
- Under **All** with no search, finished and resolved threads fold into "**N done** or resolved".
- With no match: "Nothing matches “q”."

## The composer

The composer sits at the bottom of every conversation. Its placeholder tells you where you are:

| Where | Placeholder |
| --- | --- |
| Home (desktop) | "What should get done? Use @project to aim it" |
| Home (phone) | "What should get done?" |
| Workspace | "Ask anything across your projects…" |
| A project | "Message project…" |
| A thread | "Reply to the agent…" |
| A resolved thread | "Reply to reopen it…" |

The line beside it, **Aim at a project (@)**, says where your message will go:

- "To the **Workspace** coordinator · @ to aim at a project" on Home and in the Workspace.
- "To the **X** coordinator" in a project.
- "Reply in **this thread**" in a thread.
- "New work for **X**" when you have typed `@` and a project name.
- "No project called **x**" when the name doesn't match a project.

### Aim with @project

Type `@` to open a list of your projects, each marked "N working" or "quiet". Use ↑ and ↓ to move, **Enter** or **Tab** to pick, and **Esc** to close. Only a real project name (or the Workspace) counts. The `@project` part is taken out of your text before it's sent.

In a thread, `@project` sends your words to that project as new work instead of replying in the thread.

### Sending

- **Enter** sends, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line, and **Esc** leaves the box.
- Your message appears at once marked **Sending**. If you retry, it is never posted twice.
- Once it arrives, the last message shows **Sent ✓**. If you sent it from another view, a line says "Sent to X · open to follow the reply".
- When a coordinator answers while you are looking elsewhere, a line says "The coordinator replied" or "X coordinator replied".
- Whatever you were typing is kept as a draft for each view.

If a message can't be sent, it shows a short title with **Retry** and **Edit**. **Edit** puts the text back in the box, `@project` included.

| Title | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| "The router is off" | New messages to the Workspace and projects are turned off in **Settings → Routing**. |
| "This project was removed" | The project no longer exists in XO. |
| "The agent isn't connected" | Claude Code isn't signed in. |
| "No agent can run this" | No agent that can work here is set up. |
| "The coordinator is busy" | The coordinator was in the middle of a handover. Send again. |
| "This thread is gone" | The thread no longer exists. |
| "XO's chat engine isn't running" | XO Space is starting up or has stopped. |
| "No answer from XO" | XO didn't answer in time. |
| "Not supported by this agent" | The agent can't do this yet. |
| "Refused by the proxy" | The page you're using isn't allowed to send this. Reload Cowork. |
| "Can't reach XO" | Your connection or XO Space is down. |

If a coordinator runs into a problem while answering, a line says "The coordinator couldn't answer: …".

For what to do about each one, see [Troubleshooting](/docs/agents/xo-cowork/troubleshooting).

### Stop

When the box is empty and something is running, the send button turns into **Stop**: "Stop the coordinator" or "Stop the agent (it keeps what's done)".

1. For 3 seconds nothing happens yet. A line shows "Stopping · …" with **Undo**.
2. Then the work stops.
3. The line reports "Stopped · … · what it did is kept", "It had already finished", or "Couldn't stop it" with **Retry**.

## Model chip

The chip beside the composer shows which model routes or answers in this conversation. Click it, or press **⌘.** (**Ctrl+.** on Windows and Linux). It isn't shown in Chats.

### In the Workspace or a project

The chip reads **Auto · Router**, **Auto · Off**, the name of a decision model, or a model you chose, with dots for how deeply it thinks.

The popover has two parts:

1. **Who routes a new message**: **The Space's** (whatever is set in **Settings → Routing**), **Coordinator**, any decision model you've connected, or **Off**. This choice applies to this conversation only.
2. **What the coordinator runs**: "Its coordinator runs on its default. New threads still get their own choice from the router." Pick a model, and a depth from **Quick** to **Deepest**, to keep the coordinator on it.

The footer reads "Applies to … from its next turn." with **Back to Auto**.

### In a thread

The chip reads **Auto ·** and the model the thread runs on, or the model you chose. The popover says "The router chose X for it." You can pick another model and depth, or choose **Back to the CLI's default**.

### What a change does

A change applies from the agent's next reply. A quiet line records it, such as "This thread now runs X · Deep, from its next turn", with a 3-second **Undo**. If it doesn't save, the line reads "Nothing changed: …".

Choosing a model for a coordinator doesn't change the threads it starts: each thread keeps its own choice.

In the popover, **A** goes back to Auto; ↑ and ↓ choose the model, ← and → the depth, and **Enter** keeps your choice.
