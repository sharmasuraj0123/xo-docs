<Lead>
  XO Cowork lets one person direct many AI agents across many projects. You say what should get done, agents do the work in the background, and Cowork shows you only what needs you. This quickstart gets Cowork running in a space on XO, then walks you through your first session.
</Lead>

## What you'll need

- An [XO account](https://app.xo.builders)
- A Claude account to sign in to **Claude Code**. Cowork runs its agents on Claude Code today.

## Create a space and open Cowork

Cowork comes with your XO space. You can create the space from either the **Claude Code** or the **XO Cowork** template, because both include Cowork. Once the space is running, Cowork is one of the apps on its home screen.

![A space's home screen, with the Cowork icon highlighted](/images/cowork/xo-cowork-quickstart-00-space-home.webp)

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create a new project

    Go to [app.xo.builders](https://app.xo.builders) and click **New Project** in the left sidebar. On the **Claude Code** or **XO Cowork** template card, click **Create Agent**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Name it

    Enter a unique **Project Name**, for example `xo`. The other fields are optional, and the default volume size is fine for most setups. Click **Create Project**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Wait for startup

    Your space takes about **5–6 minutes** to start. When it's ready, open your space. You'll see its home screen, like the one above.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Open Cowork

    On the home screen, click the **Cowork** icon.

    <Callout type="info">
      Cowork's browser tab reads "XO Space" and its sidebar reads **Space**.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Connect Claude Code

    If the line above the message box reads "Claude isn't connected · connect it in XO · sending waits until then", sign in to Claude Code from XO Space's setup, under **Setup → Intelligence layer**. Anything you send waits, then starts as soon as Claude Code is connected.

    If you used the **Claude Code** template, you can also connect Claude from its setup checklist. See [Connect Claude](/docs/agents/claude-code/setup#connect-claude).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Start on Home

![Home in XO Cowork, with the sidebar on the left and Your next move on the right](/images/cowork/xo-cowork-quickstart-01-home.webp)

Home answers one question: what is worth your time right now, across every project? It opens with the date, a greeting and a count, such as "Six things are worth your time." When something needs you, **Your next move** shows the one most worth doing first.

The sidebar holds:

- **Create XO Project**: start a new project.
- **Home**: what is worth your time, with a count when something needs you.
- **Workspace**: your coordinator across all projects.
- **Chats**: direct chats with one agent in one folder.
- **Projects**: one row per project. When a project needs you, its row shows a count and its most important ask.

<Callout type="info">
  The screenshots on this page come from **Demo** mode, so the projects in them are examples. To explore safely yourself, switch the badge at the bottom-left of the sidebar to **Demo**. Nothing you do there reaches a real agent.
</Callout>

## Ask: hand off work

Say what you want once, where it belongs. Cowork gets it to the right agent.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create your first project

    Click **Create XO Project**. Give it a **Project name** and, if you like, a sentence or two under **What is it for?** Cowork creates a project folder with a short `AGENTS.md` file. Add your own instructions for agents there, such as how to build and test the project.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Write to the Workspace

    Not sure where something belongs? Open **Workspace** and write to it. Its coordinator sees every project, answers questions across them, and passes work to the right project. Try "What projects do I have?" to check that everything is connected.

    ![The Workspace coordinator answering a question across projects](/images/cowork/xo-cowork-quickstart-02-workspace.webp)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Aim at a project with @

    Or aim it yourself. Type `@` and pick a project from the list. The line under the message box changes to **New work for** and the project's name, and your message goes straight there as new work.

    ![Typing @ in the message box to pick a project](/images/cowork/xo-cowork-quickstart-03-aim.webp)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Let the project start a thread

    Each project has its own coordinator. It answers quick questions itself and starts a **thread** for any change. A thread card appears in the conversation with its progress, current step, branch and **Open →**.

    ![A project's coordinator starting a thread for a change](/images/cowork/xo-cowork-quickstart-04-project.webp)
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Follow the thread

    A thread is one piece of work, with its own agent, its own branch and a live checklist: ✓ done, ✱ in progress, ○ to do. Open it to see what the agent is doing, or reply to talk to the agent directly.

    ![A thread with its checklist, branch and agent messages](/images/cowork/xo-cowork-quickstart-05-thread.webp)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Decide: answer only what needs you

Agents keep working on their own. When one needs a decision, it waits for you in one place.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Take your next move

    Home ranks what is waiting and leads with **Your next move**. Each ask shows how long it takes (**Glance ~5 s** or **Decide ~1 min**), where it came from, how much agent work waits on it, and what the agent suggests. Pick an answer, open **Details**, or **Send back** with a note.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Work through Needs you

    Decisions come one at a time in the **Needs you** panel. **Allow** each one, or **Send back** with a note. Skip freely: nothing in the panel has to be answered first.

    ![The Needs you panel beside Home, showing one decision at a time](/images/cowork/xo-cowork-quickstart-06-needs-you.webp)

    <Callout type="warn">
      Risky asks are marked **Destructive**. To allow one, you hold the button instead of clicking, so a stray click never allows it.
    </Callout>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Make it a rule

    Answer the same thing often? Turn on **Make it a rule**, and **Allow** becomes **Allow + rule**. Choose how far the rule reaches, such as **Exactly this command** or any `npm install`. Cowork shows the exact sentence it will save, word for word.

    ![Make it a rule, showing the exact rule that will be saved](/images/cowork/xo-cowork-quickstart-07-rule.webp)

    Only you can make rules. See and revoke them in **Settings → Rules**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each answer clears one ask from Home. Everything else keeps working without you.
