<Lead>The quirq page defined the meter. This note builds it: the environment that makes minting trustworthy, the full calculus, the company ledger, the attacks, and the hypothesis-first validation program.</Lead>

**XO Research** · July 2026 (v3)

**TL;DR:** Quirqs are only as real as the machinery beneath them. The
environment is that machinery: it captures state, meters cost, and writes the
record, which is why it, not the agent, is the load-bearing component. On top of
it, one ledger yields every number an operator needs (cost per quirq, QER, the
intervention rate) and their trajectories over time. A unit of account invites
gaming, so the attacks and mitigations are catalogued, and every empirical claim
is stated with a falsifier and bound to a numbered experiment.
