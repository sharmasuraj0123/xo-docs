  Connect your ChatGPT subscription to a Codex or OpenClaw space. XO Cloud shows a

## What you need

- A space whose status reads **Ready** (see [Your space](/docs/cloud/your-space)).
- A ChatGPT account with a subscription that includes Codex.

<Callout type="warn">
  Device code login must be enabled in your ChatGPT security settings. The dialog
  links to them.
</Callout>

## Connect your ChatGPT subscription

1. Open the space's **Setup** page and choose **Models**.
2. Under **OAUTH SUBSCRIPTIONS**, choose **Connect** on the **OpenAI** row.

   ![Setup › Models in a Codex space with the OpenAI row and its Connect highlighted](/images/cloud/setup-models-openai.png)

3. The **Connect ChatGPT subscription** dialog opens with a one-time code under
   **2 Enter this one-time code**. The copy button next to it copies it.
4. Under **1 Open ChatGPT sign-in**, choose **Open ChatGPT sign-in**. OpenAI's
   device sign-in page opens in a new tab.
5. Sign in with the account that has your subscription and enter the one-time code.

   ![The Connect ChatGPT subscription dialog with the one-time code highlighted, waiting for you to enter it](/images/cloud/setup-codex-connect.png)

While you sign in, the dialog reads "Waiting for you to enter the code…". When the
subscription is connected, the **OpenAI** row shows a green check mark. Close the
dialog with **Close**.
